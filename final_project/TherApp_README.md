
# TherApp 🧠🌿 

**Your daily mental health companion**  

## About TherApp  
TherApp(Short for therapy) was born out of a passion for mental well-being, to make it accessible to everyone.This app is made to help users track their emotions, practice mindfulness, and explore therapeutic exercises. It aims to provide a safe space for self-reflection, stress relief and relaxation.

---

## Features  

1. **Mood Tracker** 😌  
   - Log your mood daily(or hourly) with a rating scale from 1-5.  
   - Add optional notes for self analysis .  
   - View an analysis of your emotional trends.

2. **Journal** 📝  
   - Save your thoughts and feelings in a private journal.  
   - Access your latest entries for self-reflection.

3. **Self-Care Tips** 🌸  
   - Discover practical and yet easy coping strategies for managing stress.

4. **Guided Exercises** 🌱🫁  
   - **Grounding Exercise:** 5-step technique to reduce overwhelm.  
   - **Breathing Exercise:** A guided routine for relaxation.

5. **Daily Affirmations** ✨  
   - Get a new motivational quote every day (just by opening the app) to boost your confidence.

5. **Online Meditation Resources** 🧘   
   - The Meditation Resources feature in TherApp offers a  free, relaxing content to support your mindfulness journey.

---

## How It Works  

1. **Launch the App:**  
   - On startup, you'll be greeted with a daily affirmation.  

2. **Navigate the Menu:**  
   - Choose from various options like logging your mood, adding journal entries, meditation e.t.c.  

3. **Track Your Progress:**  
   - Access detailed mood analytics and journal insights to understand your mental health better.  

4. **Interactive Exercises:**  
   - Follow breathing or grounding techniques with clear instructions to reduce anxiety.  

---

## Usage Guide  

1. **Log a Mood:**  
   - Select "Track your Mood" from the menu.  
   - Rate your mood and add optional notes.  

2. **Write in Journal:**  
   - Select "Write in Journal" and enter your thoughts.  

3. **Practice Mindfulness:**  
   - Try exercises from the menu for instant stress relief.  

4. **Self-Care Tips:**  
   - Get random suggestions for improving mental well-being.  
5. **Online Meditation Resources:**  

   - You Can just choose which link you want to open and app will launch it for you
---

## Installation  

1. Ensure Python 3.8+ is installed on your device.  
2. Save the script files (`therapp.py`,`journal.py`,`mood_tracker.py`,`file_handler.py`) and ensure the dependencies (`json`, `time`, etc.) are available. 
3. Also take a look to requirements.txt file
4. Run the application

## Important Note  
TherApp  is not a substitute for professional therapy, medical advice, or treatment. The information and resources provided within TherApp are intended to encourage personal growth and self-awareness but should not be relied upon as a solution for serious mental health issues.
